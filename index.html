        How to Play

        • Everyone starts off the board.
        • Roll the die and move one square per pip.
        • Land at the bottom of a ladder → climb up. Land on a snake's head → slide down. At most one per turn.
        • First to reach square 100 wins. By default you must land exactly on 100; too high a roll and you stay put (the bounce option bounces you back instead).
        • Modes: Player vs Computer, Local Multiplayer (2–4 players sharing turns) and Solo practice.
